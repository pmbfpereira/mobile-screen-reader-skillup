Mobile Screen Reader Skill-up

Hands-on exercises for exploring mobile web content with VoiceOver on iPhone or TalkBack on Android.

Exercise 1

Navigation & semantics

Practice navigating a mobile page and identifying problems with headings, names, grouping, reading order, statuses and repeated actions.

 Exercise 2

Interaction & focus

Practice forms, validation, dialogs, focus movement, dynamic announcements and a web-content transition.
How to use these exercises
  • Turn on VoiceOver or TalkBack.
  • Try the bad example first.
  • Discuss what made the task difficult.
  • Switch to the improved example and compare.

These are mobile-web accessibility exercises, not simulations of native iOS or Android accessibility APIs.
